# Problem Statement: Crypto Trading for Students in India

## The Problem
Indian students (18–25) are increasingly curious about crypto, but have no safe, affordable, or educational way to start. They learn from YouTube/Telegram "gurus," risk real money on hype, and get hit by scams, high taxes, and confusing regulation.

## Who Is Affected
- College students with small budgets (₹500–₹5,000 to invest)
- Beginners with no finance background
- Students in tier 2/3 cities with limited access to quality financial education

## Pain Points
1. **Regulatory confusion** – 30% flat tax on gains, 1% TDS on transfers, no loss set-off; unclear legal status.
2. **Scams & misinformation** – pump-and-dump groups, fake exchanges, "guaranteed return" influencers.
3. **Lack of education** – no beginner-friendly learning on risk, volatility, wallets, security.
4. **Emotional/FOMO trading** – students lose money by buying peaks and panic selling.
5. **KYC/onboarding friction** – many students lack PAN, or have limited banking/UPI access.
6. **No safe practice** – no way to trade without risking real money first.
7. **High fees on small amounts** – minimum order sizes and fees hurt small investors.

## Opportunity
A student-focused platform that combines **education + paper-trading simulation + tax awareness + scam protection**, optionally leading to small-ticket real investing via a regulated partner exchange.

## Core Questions to Validate
- Do students actually want this, and will they pay (or is it free/ad/partner-funded)?
- Is an education/simulator product legally safe vs. offering trading itself?
- Which partner exchanges (WazirX, CoinDCX, CoinSwitch, etc.) offer APIs/affiliate programs?
- What age/KYC limits apply to under-18 or non-PAN users?

## Success Metrics
- 1,000 active student users in first 3 months
- 50% completion of beginner course
- Weekly active rate > 25%
- Measurable drop in self-reported risky behaviour (survey)

## Risks
- Regulatory change or ban
- Being seen as giving financial advice (SEBI/RBI issues)
- Partner exchange dependency
- Market crash reducing interest

## Disclaimer
Educational only. Not financial advice.
